How it works

Three steps, and the hard part is ours.

  1. 1

    Send an event

    Call the API or the SDK once. Relay stores the event and answers in milliseconds.

  2. 2

    We retry and sign it

    Relay signs the request and delivers it, retrying with backoff until the endpoint says yes.

  3. 3

    You check the log

    Every attempt is in the delivery log, with the response, so support questions take a minute.
