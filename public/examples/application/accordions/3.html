How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within one working day. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 days and express takes 1 to 2 days.

Can I return an item?

Return anything unused within 30 days. We email a prepaid label and refund you when the parcel arrives.

Do you ship internationally?

We ship to 38 countries. Duties are shown at checkout, so there are no surprise charges on delivery.
