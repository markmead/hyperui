What is included in the free plan?

Three projects, 5 GB of storage and up to 5 teammates. No card is needed to sign up.

Is my data backed up?

We back up every workspace each night and keep the copies for 30 days. Restores take about 15 minutes.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Add a CNAME record, paste your domain into Settings and we issue the certificate for you.
