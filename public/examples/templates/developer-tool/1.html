How it works

Three steps, and the hard part is ours.

  1. 1

    Send an event

    Call the API or the SDK once. Tidehook stores the event and answers in milliseconds.

  2. 2

    We retry and sign it

    Tidehook signs the request and delivers it, retrying with backoff until the endpoint says yes.

  3. 3

    You check the log

    Every attempt is in the delivery log, with the response, so support questions take a minute.

Everything between send and received

The parts of webhook delivery that every team writes, gets wrong once, and then maintains forever.

Retries with backoff

Failed deliveries retry for up to three days, with exponential backoff and jitter so a slow endpoint can recover.

HMAC signing

Every request carries a SHA-256 signature and a timestamp, so receivers can reject forged or replayed events.

Replay

Fixed a bug in your handler? Replay one event, or every event from the last hour, from the dashboard or the API.

Delivery logs

See the request, the response and the timing of every attempt, searchable for 30 days on every plan.

Fan-out

Send one event to many endpoints. Each endpoint gets its own retries, so one slow receiver never blocks the rest.

Rate limits

Set a per-endpoint limit, and Tidehook queues the overflow instead of knocking your customer's server over.

Verify a signature in 5 lines

Every request is signed with your endpoint's secret. The SDK checks the signature and the timestamp, and returns the event, or nothing if it was forged or replayed.

Questions

What counts as an event?

One call to send, whatever the number of endpoints it fans out to. Retries and replays are free.

How long do you keep retrying?

Up to three days, with exponential backoff. After that the event is marked as failed, and you can replay it at any time while it's in the log.

Can I bring my own signing secret?

Yes. Each endpoint has its own secret, and you can rotate it with a grace period where both the old and new secrets are valid.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU or the US, your choice, set per project. Event payloads are encrypted at rest.

What happens if I go over my plan?

Nothing breaks. On Free, events queue until the next month or until you upgrade. On Pro and Scale, extra events are billed per million.
