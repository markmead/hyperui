What customers say

Billing questions

Is there a free trial?

Team has a 14-day trial with every feature, and no card is needed. At the end you choose a plan or drop to Free, and nothing is deleted.

How does per-user pricing work?

You pay for people who can host meetings. People who only book time with your team are always free.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly billing?

Yes, at any time. Moving to yearly takes effect straight away, with the unused part of your month credited.

What happens if I add people mid-month?

We charge for the days they're on the plan, and the amount shows on your next invoice.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits and education?

Yes. Registered non-profits and schools get 50% off Team and Business. Contact us with proof of status.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Monthly plans stop at the end of the month. Yearly plans run to the end of the year, and we refund unused months on request.
